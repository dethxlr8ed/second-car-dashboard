    How we rank

    Tiers and the order within Top pick and Worth a look match our hand-reviewed comparison page. The score is a 0-100 position within that order. Passes are ordered by a weighted blend: automatic (biggest factor, so either of us can drive it), single owner, no reported damage, model reliability reputation, price, miles, CARFAX service records, and drive time as a small factor. Penalties: Mini N18 Cooper S engines (2011-16 Cooper S, Countryman S, Paceman S) for timing-chain risk, 2007-10 Cooper S N14 for the same, ex-police cars for hard use, older Mercedes and BMW for repair cost, the 2006 Mini base automatic for its CVT, and the 2007 Prius for hybrid battery age.

    Drive times and distances are from Fremont city center, by OSRM routing over OpenStreetMap, without traffic. Expect longer at rush hour. All 30 listings are dealer sales per CARFAX. Prices, miles and history are as CARFAX showed them on Nov 13, 2026; confirm with the dealer before driving out.

    Running costs

    Yearly cost = fuel + insurance (estimate) + tires + maintenance + registration, at 10,000 miles a year. The bar on each card splits it: Fuel Insurance (est.) Tires Maintenance Registration and smog. Bars share one scale, so a longer bar costs more per year.

    3-year total = price + 10.25% Fremont sales tax + DMV transfer and registration + $85 doc fee + $225 pre-purchase inspection + any immediate check (for example a Prius battery scan) + three years of running costs. Resale value is not subtracted. Insurance is an estimate (California minimum coverage plus average comprehensive, scaled by model) and will vary by driver. Fuel uses AAA Oakland prices for Nov 12-13, 2026: $5.62 regular, $6.10 premium. Tire sets are Tire Rack prices for a budget and a premium all-season, before installation; the yearly tire cost spreads a budget set plus install over its mileage warranty. The five manual cars were not costed and show a dash.
